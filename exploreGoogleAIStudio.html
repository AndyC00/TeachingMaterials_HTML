What is a Prompt?

A prompt is the instructions or conversation we give an AI model so it knows what we want, like talking to a smart assistant with clear directions.

  • Example prompts: "Summarize this paragraph" or "Write a 4-line poem about sunlight."
  • Prompt engineering is the skill of phrasing these instructions effectively to get the output you want.
  • It often means adding context, specifying the format you want, or giving examples the model can mimic.
